# Fighter Zero — visual identity options + concept prompt pack

Pick one direction (or mix elements). All three are original designs built for
the current gameplay kit (fireball / uppercut all-rounder), the sunset rooftop
stage, and a strong silhouette. Once you choose, we lock the palette and the
name, update the design doc, and only then start references → model sheet.

---

## Option A — KAITO CROSS, "The Rooftop Drifter"  *(recommended)*

- **Fantasy:** a free-running martial artist who trains parkour-style across the
  city's rooftops at dusk; calm, precise, always slightly amused.
- **Body/stance:** lean athletic build, upright bouncing stance on the balls of
  the feet — reads fast and technical.
- **Costume:** sleeveless indigo training jacket with a high collar, white
  hand-wraps, loose cropped trousers, flat split-toe shoes, long white headband
  tails that animate in the wind (great motion accent for idle/jumps).
- **Silhouette hooks:** headband tails + high collar + tapered trousers.
- **Palette seed:** indigo `#3d7dd8` family, off-white wraps, warm skin, a single
  ember-orange accent (matches the sunset stage without vanishing into it).
- **Why it fits:** motion-focused design suits the shoto kit; the current blue
  procedural look transitions naturally.

## Option B — MARA FLINT, "The Foundry Champion"

- **Fantasy:** ex-factory welterweight who fought her way out of the industrial
  district; blunt, stubborn, all forward pressure.
- **Body/stance:** compact powerful build, square boxing stance, chin tucked.
- **Costume:** cropped hi-vis orange work jacket (sleeves rolled), heavy bandaged
  fists, steel-toe boots, short undercut hair, dog tags.
- **Silhouette hooks:** boxy jacket vs. bare forearms; boots widen the base.
- **Palette seed:** safety-orange + steel grey + charcoal.
- **Note:** punch-first silhouette slightly fights the kick-heavy kit; strongest
  as **character 2** with her own rushdown frame data.

## Option C — OSKAR BRAND, "The Last Doorman"

- **Fantasy:** veteran nightclub doorman of a demolished venue; slow, immense,
  courteous until the bell.
- **Body/stance:** huge and heavy, low wide stance, hands open.
- **Costume:** worn long overcoat over a waistcoat, loosened tie, taped knuckles.
- **Silhouette hooks:** coat mass + shaved head; widest silhouette in the game.
- **Note:** built for a future **grappler** — wrong body for Fighter Zero's speed.
  Keep on file for character 3.

## Concept prompt pack (for ChatGPT / Gemini / Midjourney)

**Rules first:** never name a real game, character, or franchise in a prompt;
outputs are references only (nothing ships); log experiments nowhere near
`public/assets/`.

**Base template** — fill the brackets:

> Original 2D fighting game character concept art, full body, side-on fighting
> stance facing right, [BODY TYPE], [COSTUME DESCRIPTION], [3–4 COLOUR PALETTE],
> confident expression, 16-bit era pixel art style, large readable silhouette,
> plain dark background, no text, no logos.

**Option A filled:**

> Original 2D fighting game character concept art, full body, side-on fighting
> stance facing right, lean athletic male martial artist on the balls of his feet,
> sleeveless indigo high-collar training jacket, white hand-wraps, loose cropped
> trousers, split-toe shoes, long white headband with flowing tails, indigo and
> off-white palette with one ember-orange accent, confident calm expression,
> 16-bit era pixel art style, large readable silhouette, plain dark background,
> no text, no logos.

**Iteration prompts** (reply to a result you half-like):

- "Same character, lower wider stance, hands higher in a guard."
- "Same character, three alternate colour schemes side by side."
- "Same character as a solid black silhouette only — I want to check readability."
- "Same character, back view and front view on one sheet, same proportions."
- "Simplify the costume: remove [element], strengthen the headband tails."

**Model-sheet prep prompt** (after picking a winner):

> Turn this design into a clean character model sheet: front, side and back view,
> T-pose and fighting stance, consistent proportions, flat colours only, no
> shading, plain white background.

The AI model sheet is still only a *reference* — the real locked-palette model
sheet is drawn by hand in Aseprite (or commissioned) per docs/ART_DIRECTION.md.
